How Synthetic-Voice Detection Works
Scientific methodology & forensic architecture notes for evaluators.
🧠 VoxShield Dual-Engine Architecture

VoxShield implements a **dual-engine security model** to detect synthetic voice clones in real-time. When simulating a live call via the Live Call Monitor, a local browser DSP engine runs real-time FFT spectral analysis. When analyzing a call file or recording via Analyze Recording, the audio is processed by our advanced **51-Feature Hybrid Machine Learning Ensemble** running on the secure backend.

🔬 The 51-Feature Forensic Vector
Physical and spectral acoustics measured from each 1-second audio frame:
1. Cepstral Shape Features (38 Features)

Extracts 12 **Linear Frequency Cepstral Coefficients (LFCCs)** and 13 **Mel-Frequency Cepstral Coefficients (MFCCs)** along with their standard deviations. These features model the human vocal tract shape. Speech synthesis vocoders leave distinct geometric anomalies in these cepstral envelopes.

2. Glottal & Pitch Dynamics (4 Features)

Measures **Harmonic-to-Noise Ratio (HNR)**, **Pitch Consistency (standard deviation of F0)**, and short-term autocorrelation **Pitch Jitter** and **Amplitude Shimmer**. Human speech naturally fluctuates due to muscle breathing dynamics; synthetic voices are often flat ("too consistent") or artificially jittery.

3. Spectral Descriptors (7 Features)

Tracks **Spectral Centroid**, **Spectral Flatness**, **Spectral Bandwidth**, **Zero Crossing Rate (ZCR)**, **Spectral Flux**, **Spectral Rolloff**, and **Spectral Contrast**. These capture high-frequency patterns where neural synthesis engines leave "vocoder fingerprint" noise.

4. Energy Descriptors (2 Features)

Evaluates **Log Linear overall energy** and **RMS energy variance** across chunks, separating silent pauses from active voice cloning.

🤖 Ensemble Classifier & Time Weights
How our models classify the features and calculate the final risk score:
1. Hybrid Ensemble Model

Instead of a single model, VoxShield passes the 51-feature vector to three models trained on telemetry-filtered real vs. cloned speech datasets:

  • Random Forest (200 Trees): Evaluates complex feature subsets and prevents overfitting.
  • MLP Neural Network (64x32 Nodes): Learns complex non-linear combinations of vocal formants.
  • Gradient Boosting Classifier: Learns sequentially to flag the hardest spoofing patterns.

The chunk score is the average prediction probability:
Chunk Risk = (PRF + PMLP + PGBC) / 3

2. Temporal Time-Decay Weighting

When evaluating an entire call, the overall risk score is calculated by giving **linearly increasing weights to later chunks**:

weights = np.linspace(0.5, 1.0, chunks)
final_risk = np.average(chunk_scores, weights=weights)

This prevents early silent or greeting seconds from diluting the detection score, prioritizing active speech clone metrics.
